Scripps Research Visiting Student (thesis/dissertation research internship, J-1)
Scripps Research · Institute · visiting · La Jolla, California, USA · Not specified on the page; agree it with the host PI
No fixed deadline; arranged individually with a host PI
Requirements: An offer from a Scripps Research PI, who must agree to host you and contact the International Services Office (ISO) to start visa sponsorship. You must also show financial support and stay enrolled at your home university. Visa paperwork includes a passport, DS-2019, invitation letter, financial documents, SEVIS fee receipt, DS-160 and a CV. The ISO does not place students, so you must contact PIs yourself. A computational PI such as one in genomics or ML would be the target.
Eligibility: International graduate students enrolled in a degree program outside the US. The J-1 Short-term/Research Scholar category applies if you hold a bachelor's degree, are enrolled as a graduate student abroad, and get most of your funding from your home university. J-1 Intern applies to graduate students funded mainly by personal or family funds, and the ISO refers them to a third-party sponsor that charges substantial fees. A Canadian PhD student is eligible but needs a US J-1 visa.
